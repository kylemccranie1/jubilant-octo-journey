THE PIT'90

Chicago, 1990. You've got $8,000, a colored jacket and a spot in the soybean pit.
Read the crowd, scalp the ticks, and trade your way from a roach-motel studio to a Hamptons mansion — or blow up and end up on the street.

Buy low. Sell high. Don't get liquidated.
