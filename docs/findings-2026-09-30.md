# Findings: one-year backtests (2026-09-30)

> The level strategies, `run_grid.py`, `run_exits.py` and the placebo control described here
> were removed on 2026-10-01 to start strategy work fresh; the simulator was kept. The code is
> in git history at commit 7d6b131.

Setup: SPY, QQQ, AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, AMZN; SIP minute bars; trading 2025-09-29 to 2026-09-29
(bars from 2025-06-02 only warm up indicators); margin account, $25k per symbol, 1% risk per
trade. Train/test split 2026-06-08. Reproduce with `run_grid.py` and `run_exits.py`
(commands below). `results/` is not committed.

## 1. Baselines (stop 0.5 ATR, target 1.0 ATR, flat by 15:55)

| Strategy | Mode | Trades | Avg R | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| daily_weekly | fade | 1,060 | -0.011 | -0.06 to +0.04 |
| daily_weekly | break | 981 | -0.010 | -0.06 to +0.04 |
| daily_weekly | vol_gated | 1,000 | -0.014 | -0.06 to +0.03 |
| sr_zones | fade | 917 | -0.014 | -0.05 to +0.03 |
| sr_zones | break | 697 | -0.008 | -0.05 to +0.04 |
| sr_zones | vol_gated | 727 | -0.033 | -0.08 to +0.01 |

No edge. Volatility gating is no better than always fading or always breaking.

## 2. Filters (feature buckets)

54 of 224 buckets beat the baseline in both train and test (24%), about the 25% expected by
chance. One bucket reaches z > 2 before correcting for 224 comparisons: sr_zones fade with
20-day realized vol 0.17-0.27 (71 test trades). Next: daily_weekly break with rv20 > 0.26 (z 1.65).
Both are candidates to test on 2021 to mid-2025, not findings.

## 3. Exits

Sweep 1 (28 configs: stop 0.15-1.0 x target 0.25-1.0/none, and 3-5 day holds):
- Train/test rank correlation of configs is -0.18 to 0.12: exit choice does not carry over.
- Day-trade exits lose in almost every cell; tight stops (0.15-0.25 ATR) are worst.
- Multi-day holds look positive only through longs riding a rising market (longs +$106k,
  shorts -$52k summed over configs). Buy and hold returned SPY +15%, QQQ +23%, AAPL +30%,
  MSFT -1%, NVDA +25%, AMZN +11%, well above any config.
- Intraday the reverse: longs lost, shorts made a little.

Sweep 2 (stop 0.5; trailing, breakeven, time stops), totals over the 4 strategy/modes:
target 1.0 -$6.1k; no target -$5.7k; trail 0.25/0.5/1.0 -$15.1k/-$7.9k/-$5.1k;
breakeven 0.25/0.5 (target 1.0) -$10.3k/-$6.9k; time stop 30/60/120 min -$5.8k/-$3.2k/-$2.1k.
Tight trails and early breakeven are worst; shorter time in the market loses least, as
expected when entries have no edge. Only positive-in-both-periods cell: daily_weekly break
with a 60-min time stop (+$1.4k), about 0.25 standard errors from zero.

## Conclusion

On this year, no exit rescues these entries, and the metrics tested don't reliably pick
better trades. Next useful checks: the two rv20 candidates on 2021 to mid-2025 (data for SPY,
QQQ, AAPL is cached); a random-entry control with the same exits, to measure what level
entries add over chance.

## 4. Previous-day high/low breaks only, positions held overnight (2026-10-01)

Scope: daily_weekly in break mode, pdh/pdl only (weekly levels and S/R zones dropped). Long =
break above the prior day's high, short = break below the prior day's low. Hold limits count
trading days; `hold=none` means exit only at the stop or target; positions open at the end of
the data are closed at the last price.

Sweep (78 configs: stop 0.25/0.5/1.0 x target 1/2/3/none x hold eod/1/3/5/10/none, plus
trailing stops 0.5/1/2 ATR): train/test rank correlation 0.12. A 0.5 ATR stop with 1-5 day
holds does best (top: stop 0.5, target 2, hold 3 days, +$7.6k over 6 x $25k, both sides
positive), but 0.25 and 1.0 stops mostly lose, and no single config is significant (best
per-trade z = 1.2, the best of 78). Buy and hold returned +15% to +30% on four of the six names.

Placebo control (`placebo_daily_weekly`): same orders and exits, but each day's levels sit at
the distance from the open that the real levels had on a random other day. 8 configs (stop 0.5,
target 2/none, hold eod/3d/10d/none) x 5 seeds:

| Side | Real beats placebo |
|---|---|
| Short (break below prior-day low) | 37 / 40 |
| Long (break above prior-day high) | 14 / 40 |
| Net | 29 / 40 |

Real shorts beat placebo shorts by roughly 1.5-2 placebo standard deviations in most configs;
real long breakouts did no better (mostly worse) than random long entries. The 3-day-hold
profits are mostly drift: the placebo also makes about +$4.4k there, nearly all from longs.

Caveats: the 8 configs share the same entries, so the 40 comparisons are not independent; only
5 placebo seeds; one year; and the short-side pattern was found in this data. Next: test the
hypothesis "prior-day-low breakdowns beat placebo shorts" on 2021 to mid-2025, untouched so far.

```bash
python run_exits.py --strategies daily_weekly --modes break --levels pdh pdl --configs '<json>' --out results/break_pd_1y
python run_exits.py --strategies placebo_daily_weekly --modes break --levels pdh pdl \
    --configs '[{"stop_atr":0.5,"target_atr":2,"hold_days":3,"placebo_seed":1}, ...]' --out results/break_pd_placebo_1y
```
